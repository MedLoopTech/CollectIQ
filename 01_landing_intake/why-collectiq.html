COMMON OBJECTIONS

Why not just...

...automate email reminders?

Sending "your invoice is overdue" is easy. Knowing who should receive it today, what happened previously, whether they promised to pay, whether it's disputed, and whether the problem is actually internal is harder. That's where CollectIQ is focused.

...use an AR dashboard?

Dashboards are useful, but finance teams often already know "AR is too high." CollectIQ is designed to answer the next question: what should we do about it today?

...hire another collections person?

That may still be the right decision. CollectIQ isn't a blanket replacement for finance staff — it helps existing staff analyze portfolios faster, prioritize more effectively, and reduce repetitive reporting work.
