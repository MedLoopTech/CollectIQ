WHY NOT JUST...

CollectIQ focuses on the decision layer that other tools often leave manual.

...send more reminders?

Reminders do not tell you which accounts deserve attention, which promise already failed or whether the real problem is a PO, approval or invoice dispute.

...use an AR dashboard?

A dashboard tells you what happened. CollectIQ is designed to turn the AR state into a ranked recovery queue and next action.

...hire another collector?

Sometimes you should. CollectIQ helps the person you already have spend less time sorting spreadsheets, remembering promises and compiling status reports.
