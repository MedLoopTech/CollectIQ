FAQ

Before you start

Do I need to connect my ERP?

No. Start with CSV or Excel. Direct integrations are not required to prove value.

Can customer names be anonymized?

Yes. The audit can run with customer codes instead of real names.

Does CollectIQ automatically contact customers?

Not during the pilot. Customer-facing collection drafts are approval-controlled.

Is the $250 Sprint guaranteed to recover a certain amount?

No. CollectIQ identifies and manages recovery opportunities, but payment depends on the debtor, disputes, contractual issues and other circumstances.

What happens after 30 days?

Stop, extend manually, or move into Managed AR Intelligence at $300–$500/month if the operating value is clear.
