30-DAY AR RECOVERY SPRINT

Don't stop at the diagnosis.

If the AR Intelligence Audit identifies a meaningful recovery opportunity, CollectIQ supports your team through a focused 30-day sprint: work the right receivables first and remove the blockers slowing down cash collection.

WEEK 1

Prioritize

  • Starting overdue AR
  • Priority accounts
  • Existing disputes
  • Customer sensitivities
  • Do-not-contact accounts
  • Finance-team ownership
WEEK 2

Engage

  • Targeted follow-up
  • Contextual communication drafts
  • Promise capture
  • Dispute classification
  • Next-action recommendations
  • Human approval stays in control
WEEK 3

Resolve

  • Unresolved purchase orders
  • Billing discrepancies
  • Approval delays
  • Missing documentation
  • Repeatedly missed commitments
  • Internal process bottlenecks
WEEK 4

Measure

  • Starting vs. current overdue AR
  • Collections received
  • Payment promises secured & kept
  • Missed promises
  • Disputes resolved
  • Remaining priority portfolio

The outcome becomes the basis for an ongoing AR intelligence program, if there is clear value.
