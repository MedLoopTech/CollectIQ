WHAT THE SYSTEM TRACKS

A recovery command center, not another static report.

Each paid Sprint is operated against persisted account-level state so recovery work does not disappear into email threads and spreadsheets.

Action Queue

Who needs attention now, why, priority score, due date and recommended action.

Payment Promises

Amount, due date, pending / kept / partial / missed status and actual payment captured.

Disputes

Category, amount, owner, status, next action and resolution notes.

Collections

Recorded cash received, payment date, reference and linked account context.

Weekly Snapshots

Overdue AR, 60+/90+, priority pool, promises, disputes, actions and collected cash.

Exceptions

Sensitive conditions and consequential decisions surface for human review instead of being silently automated.

WEEKLY CFO RECOVERY BRIEF

What changed, what recovered, what is still blocked.

The operating engine turns actual Sprint records into a factual weekly brief. AI can improve the narrative, but persisted finance data remains the source of truth.
