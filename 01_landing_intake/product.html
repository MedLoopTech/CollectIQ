03

Customer 360

See each debtor in context: total and overdue balance, oldest invoice, average days late, payment behavior, open invoices, recent communications, payment promises, dispute history, and recommended next action — in one view instead of scattered across spreadsheets, inboxes, and notes.

04

Promise-to-Pay Intelligence

Customers often say "we'll pay on Friday." The real question is whether they did. CollectIQ tracks promised amount, promised date, pending, fulfilled, partial, and missed promises — so your team can distinguish reliable commitments from optimistic ones over time.

05

Dispute & Blocker Intelligence

Not every unpaid invoice should get another reminder. Payment may be blocked by purchase-order issues, missing documents, billing errors, approval delays, pricing discrepancies, service disputes, tax documentation, or contract questions. CollectIQ identifies and categorizes these so the right internal team can resolve them.

Sometimes the fastest path to cash isn't another collection email — it's fixing the operational problem preventing payment.

COLLECTION PRIORITY

Not all overdue dollars deserve equal effort.

A $2,000 invoice 95 days late is different from a $50,000 invoice 40 days late, with a missed payment commitment, from a strategically important customer, where nobody has followed up in two weeks.

CollectIQ's priority model is designed to capture those differences. The result: work smarter, not simply oldest-first.

AI + HUMAN FINANCE JUDGMENT

AI should make your finance team stronger — not reckless.

AI can assist with

  • Summarizing account history
  • Classifying customer replies
  • Identifying dispute categories
  • Extracting payment promises
  • Drafting follow-up communication

AI does not

  • Independently determine core financial calculations
  • Set ageing, balances, ratios, or scoring inputs
  • Act without human review on relationship-sensitive accounts, disputes, or escalation

Ageing, balances, ratios, scoring inputs, and portfolio metrics are calculated using structured finance rules. Human review remains essential during the initial service, especially for outbound communication.
