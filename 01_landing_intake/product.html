THE RECOVERY ENGINE

From ageing file to next-best recovery action.

CollectIQ keeps accounting facts deterministic, then layers AI interpretation, action design and approval-controlled recovery workflows around them.

01

Deterministic AR Audit

CollectIQ reads CSV/XLSX ageing data, maps columns, validates dates and balances, then calculates total AR, overdue exposure, ageing buckets, concentration, dispute value, missed promises, priority pool and AR Health Score. AI does not invent these figures.

02

AI Recovery Interpretation

The Audit Agent reads the structured audit and explains the recovery opportunity, why specific accounts matter, which blockers require action and what finance should do next. Its recovery estimate is bounded by deterministic audit facts.

03

Priority Accounts

Invoices are ranked using overdue age, value, promise history, customer behavior, contact recency and known blockers. The goal is not to chase every invoice — it is to focus scarce finance attention where it can matter most.

04

Promise & Dispute Intelligence

During the Recovery Sprint, CollectIQ records promises to pay, detects when a due promise becomes missed, tracks disputes and next actions, and keeps unresolved blockers visible until they are addressed.

05

Approval-Controlled Actions

Routine internal analysis can run autonomously. Customer-facing collection drafts, unusual conclusions, sensitive escalation and consequential financial actions enter an approval queue. This lets automation increase without giving up control.

Green: autonomousAmber: approvalRed: founder-controlled
06

Manager + CFO Intelligence

The operating layer summarizes outstanding approvals, exceptions, active audits and Sprint activity for the operator. Weekly CFO briefs convert Sprint records into factual recovery reporting: cash recorded, promises, missed commitments, disputes and next actions.

WHAT AI CAN AND CANNOT DO

Automate the work. Keep authority over the consequences.

AI can

  • Interpret an approved AR audit
  • Prioritize recovery opportunities
  • Summarize promises and blockers
  • Draft account follow-ups
  • Generate internal and CFO recovery briefs
  • Surface exceptions that require attention

AI cannot independently

  • Change ledger balances or fabricate financial facts
  • Guarantee recoverability
  • Send sensitive collection communications during the pilot
  • Make legal decisions or threaten escalation
  • Approve contracts, refunds or major pricing exceptions
