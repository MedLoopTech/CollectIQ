THE PROBLEM

Your AR team doesn't need more reminders. It needs better priorities.

Most receivables processes still rely on ageing spreadsheets, manually maintained collection notes, email follow-ups, individual staff memory, disconnected dispute tracking, and weekly reports assembled by hand.

The problem isn't that finance teams don't know invoices are overdue. The problem is knowing:

Which ten accounts should we work on today?
Which customers promised to pay this week?
Which promises have already been broken?
Which invoices are actually blocked internally?
Where can management intervene to release cash?

CollectIQ is designed around those questions.
