Our story

Most of what happens doesn't matter to you.

The world changes every second. Thousands of things happen — a company posts a job, a grant opens, a fund makes a move, a customer shows a sign of strain. Almost none of it is relevant to what you're actually trying to do.

Aimfold helps you focus on the few signals that can move you forward: it watches continuously, gathers evidence, scores what it finds, and explains exactly why each opportunity matters and why now — so you spend your attention on the handful of things worth acting on, not the noise around them.
